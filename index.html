Call Sprint kaise chalayein?

1. Leads daalo: PDF upload karo jisme naam/number hon — app khud numbers nikaal ke ek tap-to-call list bana degi. Purani list ke saath naye numbers merge ho jayenge, purana data delete nahi hoga.

2. Call karo: Kisi bhi lead pe tap karo, direct call/WhatsApp button milega. Call ke baad status daalo — Pending, Picked, Busy, ya No Answer.

3. Lead score karo: Har contact ko Hot 🔥 (turant follow-up), Warm 🌤️ (interested), ya Cold ❄️ (abhi interested nahi) mark karo. Hot leads hamesha upar dikhengi.

4. Filter/track karo: Upar ke chips se Pending, Picked ya Hot leads filter karo. Stats (Pending/Picked/Busy count) hamesha dikhte rahenge.

5. Premium (₹49/30 din): Unlimited leads, PDF export, aur auto-dialer jaise features unlock karne ke liye "⭐ Premium Recharge" button use karo.

1. Add leads: Upload a PDF with names/numbers — the app extracts them into a tap-to-call list. New numbers merge with your existing list; nothing old gets deleted.

4. Filter & track: Use the chips at the top to filter Pending, Picked, or Hot leads. Live stats (Pending/Picked/Busy counts) are always visible.
